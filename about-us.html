About Manchester City

From a church team in Gorton to a global football force.

Our Club

Manchester City Football Club is one of the most successful clubs in English football. Based in the east of Manchester, the club plays its home games at the Etihad Stadium and wears its famous sky blue.

Over more than a century, City has gone through glory, heartbreak and reinvention, and that journey is what makes the club so special to its supporters around the world.

Club at a Glance
  • Founded: 1880
  • Nickname: The Citizens / Man City
  • Colours: Sky blue & white
  • Stadium: Etihad Stadium
  • League: Premier League

Our History

1880
St. Mark's (West Gorton)

The club begins life as a church team, created to give local people something positive to do.

1894
Manchester City is Born

After becoming Ardwick AFC, the club is reformed and takes the name Manchester City.

1904
First Major Trophy

City win the FA Cup, the first big honour in the club's history.

1968
League Champions

Under Joe Mercer and Malcolm Allison, City win the First Division title, followed by the FA Cup and a European trophy.

2003
A New Home

The club moves from Maine Road to the City of Manchester Stadium, today known as the Etihad Stadium.

2008
New Ownership

Abu Dhabi United Group takes over, beginning a period of huge investment in the squad, stadium and academy.

2012
That Last-Minute Title

Sergio Agüero scores in stoppage time on the final day to win the Premier League, one of the most dramatic finishes ever.

2023
The Treble

City win the Premier League, FA Cup and UEFA Champions League in the same season, a first for the club.

The Etihad Stadium

City's home since 2003, the Etihad has grown into one of the best stadiums in the country, hosting league games, European nights and major events. Next to it sits the club's training and academy complex, where the next generation of players develops.
